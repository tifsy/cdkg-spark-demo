**Review Guidelines** (fallback copy; the live queue's own guidelines are used when available)

Decide for each source whether it stays in the collection.

**Keep** when the outlet publishes its own original news reporting, shows an identifiable editorial team or journalists, has a clear organisational identity (e.g. an About page), and the site is active.

**Remove** when the site mainly republishes other outlets' articles (aggregator), is a blog, PR channel or company website without an editorial function, publishes satire or unreliable content, or is no longer active.

**Skip** when you are unsure and want to decide later.
